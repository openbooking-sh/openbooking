Waitlist offer email. `attachWaitlistNotifications({ waitlist, mailer, config })` emails the customer when a slot is held for them from the waitlist (what, when, until when, a link to confirm, the cancellation policy), once per offer, honouring unsubscribes. `renderWaitlistOfferEmail` is the template.
